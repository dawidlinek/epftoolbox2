Before the library wrapped a family, the research ran it with its own scripts on the recent DE_LU and AT data and stored every
forecast. This page holds the library's runs of the same families against those stored forecasts, in three tiers:

- **Tier A** (in CI, exact): the library's report, `AverageEnsemble` and `DMEvaluator` rebuild the research's leaderboard from
  the stored forecasts themselves, every row within 1e-9 (`tests/test_zoo_leaderboard.py`, from the fixture in
  `tests/fixtures/zoo/`). It checks the path from records to the table, not the models.
- **Tier B1** (outside CI, per value): the library's model on the research's own fits (the same rows, the same refit days, the
  same calls) against the stored forecast of each key.
- **Tier B2** (outside CI, per year): the library's model with its own defaults and calendar over each year, its MAE against
  the stored member's on identical keys.

<Aside type="note">
The research's scripts and the library's runs are in the project's research folder (`library_check_step6/`, one subfolder per
step-6 branch and `families_scale/` for this page); none of it runs in CI. The data, the weights and an H100 are needed.
</Aside>

## Setup

- **Data.** `recent/data/{DE_LU,AT}.npz` of the research: hourly day-ahead prices, the TSO load forecast (latest revision) and the
  wind and solar forecast, 2021-01-02 to 2026-10-11, in the research's DST-resolved layout (every local day 24 hours).
- **Frame.** That layout on a fixed-offset `Etc/GMT-1` clock (every day 24 hours; DST is tested elsewhere), with the columns of
  `presets.lear_predictors(["load_forecast"])` in the research's `lear_features` order (175 inputs), the weekday dummies of the
  target day, and for the trees `presets.tree_predictors(["load_forecast"])`.
- **Information set.** Issue time 11:45 with the rules given in the frame's own zone: prices of D published at 13:00 on D-1, the
  load forecast of D at 10:00 on D-1, the weekday dummies static (the built-in rules are Brussels time, which on this clock puts
  a summer day's 23:00 price after the issue). In every run `report.availability()` listed nothing late before any comparison
  (the foundation models list each series they read, every one on time; the in-context runs use `availability="raise"`,
  which refuses a late input before any fit).
- **Years.** Validation: issue days 2024-10-01 to 2025-09-29 (target days 2024-10-02 to 2025-09-30), 364 days, 8,736 forecasts.
  Test: issue days 2025-09-30 to 2026-09-29, 365 days, 8,760 forecasts. Horizon 1 (a forecast for target day t is issued on
  t - 1). Every comparison below is on identical keys, and every count is the number of forecasts both sides hold.
- **Stored forecasts** are rounded to 4 decimals, so a difference of 5e-5 EUR/MWh is the files' rounding.

| LEAR + boosted residuals | ≤ 1e-6 with the stored base | ±0.5 % with the library's base |

B2's ±0.5 % for refit families comes from the calendar: the research refitted on each year's first target day and every 7 days
after, the library on Mondays of its fixed epoch, so no fit coincides and agreement over the year is what can be asked (one test
year resolves about 5 % of the MAE). A zero-shot foundation model has no fit and no calendar: its B1 already covers every
forecast of both years, so its B2 is the same run's year MAE.

## What wasn't reproduced, and why

- **PatchTST-FM-r2, per value.** Up to 2.96 EUR/MWh from the stored forecasts on an H100 (0.07 on average), against B1's 1e-3,
  with the MAE within 0.02 % (B2 met). granite-tsfm runs the backbone under bfloat16 autocast on a GPU, so single values move with
  rounding; the research's calls held 64 consecutive days (the last one 44), the library's a calendar block of 64, padded, and the
  research ran granite-tsfm 0.3.10 with another torch. Which of these moves which value was not separated (0.3.10 against 0.3.12
  on the CPU: 1.4e-4). Recorded as not reproduced at 1e-3.
- **TabPFN v2 on a GPU, per value.** One value of the one-day last test block is 0.616 EUR/MWh from the stored one (the other
  blocks within 0.44, 0.018 on average), above the 0.5 tolerance, with the MAE within 0.005 %. The research called the model on
  that one row, the library on the block's nominal 7 rows (the row and 6 copies), and under TabPFN's float16 autocast the length
  of a call moves the last digits further than in float32 (9.3e-4 on the CPU). Recorded as the GPU's half-precision gap, not an
  adapter error; on the same device and package versions the library equals the research's own code within 4.5e-8.
- **LEAR + boosted residuals over the year.** −0.55 % (DE_LU) and +1.72 % (AT) on the test year against the 0.5 % tolerance with
  scikit-learn 1.8.0, and −0.85 % / +0.58 % with the research's 1.9.1 (step 6k): on the research's own rows the library is exact,
  so the gap is the year-level spread of the tree. The refit calendar alone moves DE_LU by +0.05 % on test, the library's LEAR
  base (0.1 to 0.25 % from the research's) by −0.4 %, another `random_state` by −0.5 to −0.8 %, and scikit-learn 1.8.0 against
  1.9.1 by −0.64 % on the research's rows: a year of this tree moves about 1 % with anything that changes its rows, more than the
  tolerance. The library's default warm-up (the base from its first full 728-day window, a year later than the research's) gives
  +1.2 % and +2.3 %.
- **The monthly t0-alpha re-fine-tune** (`t0aftroll`, the section's best t0 row) pooled DE_LU and AT windows in each fine-tune; a
  single-market run can't give it, so it waits for pooled markets. The fine-tune mechanics are reproduced (B1 above); the
  library's default yearly fine-tune is reported, not gated.
- **Not wrapped**: Mitra-v2 (its 1,000-bin head needs patches of AutoGluon's internals, see
  [In-Context Models](/epftoolbox2/models/in-context/#mitra-v2)), TFT (jointly trained on both markets), TiRex-2, Toto-2 and the
  t0-beta variants; their stored forecasts stay in the table below as stored.
- **LEAR's window average** (not a step-6 family, its gate is step 4's on Lago's data): the library's LEAR (BIC, Lago's
  coordinate-descent refit, daily, data through the issue day) is within 0.2 % of the research's port at 364 and 728 days but
  1 to 2.5 % better at 56 and 84 days (DM p < 0.05 in most of those years), so its window average is 0.7 % and 0.8 % below the
  stored LEAR_CWA on test. The research's port (`epfq`, the quality agent's) isn't the original epftoolbox, which the library
  reproduces within 6.2e-10 ([Benchmark Reproduction](/epftoolbox2/models/lear-benchmark/)); the short windows, with their few
  rows per fit, are where two implementations of the scaling and the selection part ways. Not investigated further here.

## Running it again

The scripts are in the research folder's `library_check_step6/families_scale/`: `common.py` (the frame, the information set,
the years), `incontext_b2.py`, `t0_ft_b2.py`, `tree_b2.py`, `mlp_b2.py`, `lear_b2.py`, `analyse.py` (B2 and the rebuilt table,
through the library's report, reading the stored members from `tests/fixtures/zoo/`), `wcss/` (the H100 jobs) and `collect.sh`.
The earlier steps' B1 scripts are next to them (`feat-in-context-models/`, `foundation/`, `foundation_flagged/`, `residual/`,
`gbm/`, `torch_core/`, `torch_stacks/`). Cost of this page's CPU runs on a 128-core machine at one thread per fit, both markets
and both years: LightGBM 2.7 core-hours, the hybrid 3.3 to 3.9 per warm-up (the LEAR base included), the MLP top 4 × 4 7.9, LEAR's four
windows 8.3.
